# Claude Arcade

Welcome to the Claude Arcade wiki.

Claude Arcade is an open-source Claude Code plugin that turns your terminal into a game. You
create a hero the first time you open it, and that hero grows with you: as you use Claude, spend
tokens and finish tasks, your character levels up, learns new spells and gets stronger.

It's a mix of Zork, the 8-bit games of the 80s and the virtual pets you had to look after. Every
prompt is a new quest. Every bug is a monster. Every agent Claude launches joins your party. It all
runs in the CLI, next to Claude, so it's something to play (or just watch) while you wait.

## Where it's heading

The long-term goal is a global leaderboard where players compare their heroes with everyone
else's, plus async PvP battles between heroes. Because of that, XP only ever comes from real
Claude usage, never from practice fights or purchases. Your level is a record of real work.
