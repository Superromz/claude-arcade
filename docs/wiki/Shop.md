# Shop

The Shop is where you spend the gold your hero collects from monsters and chests. Open it with
`tab` (or a click on **Shop**).

![The Shop tab showing hats, with the hero previewing a Party Hat](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Superromz/claude-arcade/main/docs/images/shop-tab.png)

It sells two kinds of things, 159 items in all:

- **Cosmetics** that show on your hero (hats, back items, auras, pets, weapon glows and trails), at
  your camp (mounts, tents, banners and campfire colors) and on your guild recruits (outfits).
- **Battle buffs** that make your hero hit harder or collect more gold for a few waves.

Nothing in the Shop gives XP, ever. XP only comes from real Claude usage, because the global
leaderboard depends on it. See [Progression and XP](Progression-and-XP).

## Getting gold

Every monster you slay drops gold, including monsters in practice waves (`w`). Tougher monsters,
elite leaders and bosses drop more, and quick chains of kills add a bonus. Every wave and every
quest also ends with a chest full of gold and loot (see [Loot and Chests](Loot-and-Chests)). Your
gold shows at the top right of the Shop, on the Hero tab and on your roster card. Each hero has its
own purse.

## Sections

The Shop is split into sections, shown as tabs along the top:

**Deals · Hats · Back · Auras · Pets · Weapon glow · Trails · Mounts · Tents · Banners · Campfires ·
Recruits · Buffs · Wardrobe**

On narrow screens the tabs scroll, with `‹` and `›` showing there are more.

## Keys

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `←` `→` | Previous or next item (and on into the next section) |
| `↑` `↓` | Move between rows, and on to the previous or next section |
| `[` `]` (or `PgUp` `PgDn`) | Jump to the previous or next section |
| `Enter` | Buy, craft, or buy today's deal. On something you own: equip it, or unequip it if it's already on |
| `u` | Unequip whatever you're wearing in the selected item's slot |
| `s` | Wardrobe: save your current look into the selected outfit |
| `x` | Wardrobe: clear the selected outfit |
| `tab` | Leave the Shop for the next tab |
| `q` | Quit the game |

While the Shop is open, the arrow keys browse items instead of switching tabs, so use `tab` or a
click to leave.

## How it works

- The left panel previews the selected item: on your hero, beside your camp's tent and fire, or on
  a row of recruits. It also shows the rarity, price, set progress and a description.
- Each card shows its price, or **Owned**, or **✓ Equipped**. A price in red means you can't afford
  it yet. Chest-only items show a recipe (**Craft ◆6 ◆2**) or **✦ Found in chests**.
- Buying a cosmetic equips it right away (with a **LOOT!** banner) and keeps it in your inventory
  for good. You can wear one item per slot.
- If you're short on gold, the footer tells you how much more you need.

![A mage wearing a crown and the Fire Aura](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Superromz/claude-arcade/main/docs/images/shop.png)

## Daily Deals

The first section, **Deals**, has three discounted cosmetics from three different slots, 20% to 50%
off. The deals change at your local midnight and are the same for every player on the same day. The
Deals tab shows how long until the next ones.

About one day in eight, the third deal is a **✦ rare deal**: a normally chest-only item, sold at 10%
under its worth. It's the only way to buy one with gold.

## Item sets

Many items belong to a set. Wear enough pieces of one set (usually 3) and you get:

- a small **battle bonus**: a few percent more damage or gold (never XP), and
- a little **set flair**: motes in the set's color circle your hero.

Camp items and recruit outfits count too, since they're part of your look. The preview panel shows
your progress (for example **Night Shift 2/3 → +5% gold**), and the Shop footer lists active set

## Camp and recruits

- **Mounts** wait at your camp while you rest, swish their tails and doze off when you do.
- **Tents** and **banners** set up at camp, and **campfires** change the color of the flames and
  their glow.
- **Recruits** outfits dress your active guild recruits (see [Companions](Companions)): a team
  color and a hat for everyone.

With nothing equipped in these slots, your camp and recruits look the same as always.

## Trails

A trail streams behind your hero while it walks or right after it attacks: stardust, flames,
bubbles, keycaps, binary, an afterimage, a comet and more.

## Wardrobe

The last section saves up to three **outfits**. An outfit is your whole look: every slot, including
camp items and the recruit outfit.

- `Enter` on an empty outfit saves your current look. `Enter` on a saved one puts it on.
- `s` saves your current look over the selected outfit, and `x` clears it.
- Pieces you no longer own are skipped. Each hero has its own wardrobe.

## Buffs

| Buff | Price | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Espresso | 30 | +15% damage for 2 waves |
| Pizza Slice | 35 | +10% damage for 5 waves |
| Whetstone | 40 | +25% damage for 3 waves |
| Piggy Bank | 45 | +25% gold for 3 waves |
| Lucky Charm | 60 | Double gold for 3 waves |
| Energy Drink | 70 | +40% damage for 1 wave |
| Treasure Map | 80 | +50% gold for 4 waves |
| War Drum | 90 | +50% damage for 2 waves |
| Hotfix Potion | 110 | +30% damage and +30% gold for 3 waves |
| Golden Keyboard | 200 | Two and a half times the gold for 3 waves |

- Buffs apply to your hero's spells and attacks (damage) and to the gold monsters drop.
- A buff counts down by one each time a wave is cleared, practice waves included. The Shop's footer
  shows your active buffs and how many waves each has left.
- Buying a buff you already have adds its waves on top. Different buffs stack: a Whetstone and a War
  Drum together give 1.25 × 1.5 damage.
- Buffs last for the current game session. They're gone if you close the game pane, so buy them
  when you're about to fight.
- Chests can also drop three chest-only buffs: Phoenix Draught, Midas Tonic and the Rubber Duck
  Oracle.
