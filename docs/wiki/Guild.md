# Guild

Companions normally leave when their agent finishes. The **Guild** lets you keep them.

## Recruiting

When one of Claude's agents finishes its job, it may ask to join your guild:

> 🤝 *Sylvara the Ranger wants to join your guild!*

Offers wait on the **Guild** tab (up to 3, newest first). Accept one to recruit it, or dismiss it.

- Recruits keep their **name** and **class** (Mage, Ranger, Knight, Warlock, Bard or Rogue),
  picked from the kind of agent they came from. See [Companions](Companions).
- Your guild holds up to **12 recruits**. Up to **3 can fight** at a time.
- Active recruits fight beside your hero in every battle and sit with you at camp.
- Each hero has their **own guild**. Switching heroes on the [Hero Roster](Hero-Roster)
  switches guilds too.

## Leveling recruits

A recruit earns **companion XP** whenever an agent of the **same class** finishes while the
recruit is fighting: +20 XP and +1 job. Level *n* needs 20·*n*·(*n*−1) companion XP.

Companion XP is separate from your hero's XP. It never adds to your hero's level or to the
leaderboard.

## Guild tab controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `↑↓←→` | Select an offer or recruit |
| `a` | Accept the selected offer (or the newest one) |
| `x` | Dismiss an offer, or release a recruit (asks to confirm) |
| `Enter` | Accept an offer, or switch a recruit between fighting and resting |
| `tab` | Next tab |
