# Bounties

The bounty board posts challenges that you complete by using Claude the way you normally do. There
are three kinds:

| Kind | On the board | Resets | Rewards |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily | 3 | Every day at midnight UTC | 20 to 110 gold |
| Weekly | 2 | Every Monday at midnight UTC | 150 to 320 gold, sometimes a buff |
| Monthly | 2 | On the 1st of every month, midnight UTC | 800 to 1,200 gold, often a strong buff |

Bounties pay **gold** and sometimes a free battle buff. They never give XP. Your level only grows
from real Claude usage (see [Progression and XP](Progression-and-XP)), which keeps the future
leaderboard fair.

## The board

The Bounties tab shows a section for each kind, with the time left until it resets. Each bounty is a
card with:

- its name and reward (◉ gold, plus a buff name when there is one)
- what to do, and a progress bar such as `3/5`
- its state: a percentage while you work on it, **★ ready to claim** when it's done, and
  **✓ claimed** once you've collected it

The header shows how many of the 7 bounties you've claimed and how many are ready.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| ← ↑ ↓ → | Select a bounty |
| Enter | Claim the selected bounty |

Everyone gets the same bounties on the same day. The board is picked from the date, not at random,
so you and a friend can race for the same ones. An unclaimed bounty disappears when its board
resets, so claim your rewards before then.

## How progress is counted

Progress comes from what you do in Claude Code: the commands Claude runs, the files it edits, the
agents it summons, the quests you finish, the chests you open, your guild, your skill tree and your
battles in the game pane. Work you did earlier the same day, week or month counts, even if the game
pane wasn't open. A few counters (output tokens, monster kills, new items, recruits and skill
ranks) count from the first time the board is shown in that period.

- A quest counts as **without a failure** when no tool call failed between your prompt and the end
  of the task.
- **Chests** count when you open them after a real wave or quest. Chests from practice waves don't
  count. "Iron chest or better" means Iron, Gold, Epic or Legendary.
- **New items** are cosmetics added to your collection, whether you bought, crafted or looted them.
- Once a bounty is done it stays done until its board resets.

## Trophies

Claimed bounties count toward the bounty trophies (Bounty Hunter, Weekly Warrior, Monthly Legend
and more). See [Progression and XP](Progression-and-XP#bounties).

## Heroes

Each hero in your [roster](Hero-Roster) has their own claimed bounties, their own bounty trophies
and their own gold.
