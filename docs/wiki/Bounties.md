# Bounties

The bounty board posts small challenges that you complete by using Claude the way you normally do.
Every day brings **3 daily bounties** and every week brings **2 weekly bounties**. Finished bounties
pay **gold**, and some weekly ones add a free battle buff.

Bounties never give XP. Your level only grows from real Claude usage (see
[Progression and XP](Progression-and-XP)), which keeps the future leaderboard fair.

## The board

The Bounties tab shows each bounty as a card with:

- its name and reward (◉ gold, plus a buff name when there is one)
- what to do, and a progress bar such as `3/5`
- its state: a percentage while you work on it, **★ ready to claim** when it's done, and
  **✓ claimed** once you've collected it

Each section shows the time left until it resets. The header shows how many bounties you've
claimed and how many are ready.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| ← ↑ ↓ → | Select a bounty |
| Enter | Claim the selected bounty |

## When bounties reset

- Daily bounties reset at midnight UTC.
- Weekly bounties reset on Monday at midnight UTC.

Everyone gets the same bounties on the same day. The board is picked from the date, not at random,
so you and a friend can race for the same ones. An unclaimed bounty disappears when its board
resets, so claim your rewards before then.

## How progress is counted

Progress comes from what you do in Claude Code: the commands Claude runs, the files it edits, the
agents it summons, the quests you finish, and your battles in the game pane. Work you did earlier
the same day (or week) counts, even if the game pane wasn't open. Output tokens are the exception: they count from the first time the board is shown that day.

A quest counts as **without a failure** when no tool call failed between your prompt and the end of
the task.
